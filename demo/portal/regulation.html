RBI & DPDP नियम

The rules, line by line.

What RBI's digital lending rules and India's data protection law ask of a loan app's outbound data, and for each one, whether VICT enforces it, gives you evidence for it, or leaves it to you.

Not legal advice. These are plain-language summaries for engineers and compliance teams. Read the sources linked below, and check obligations with your counsel.

The map

Obligation by obligation.

Enforced VICT stops the violation in the request path. Evidence VICT gives you a record to show. Partial covers part of it. Not covered is still your job.

ObligationWhat VICT doesWhat it leaves in the ledgerCoverage
Use borrower data only with explicit consent, for a stated purpose.RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025 · DPDP Act, 2023 A consent token is issued per lender, borrower and purpose. No valid consent, no token, no call. token-issued and token-denied, each with its purpose. Enforced
Withdrawal must be as easy as giving consent, and processing must stop.DPDP Act, 2023 · DPDP Rules, 2025 Withdrawal blocks the next call and closes connections already open for that consent, within a second. consent-withdrawn blocks, and tunnel-closed with the bytes moved before the cut. Enforced
Share data only with agreed parties, for the agreed function.RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025 A vendor allow-list, plus the hosts named inside each token. A rogue SDK or a host swap is refused. host-not-allow-listed, host-not-in-token, raw-ip-target. Enforced
Keep data in India. Data processed abroad must be deleted there and brought back within 24 hours.RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025 · RBI payment data circular, 2018 Terraform refuses non-India AWS regions. Calls to hosts outside the vendor list, including ones abroad, are refused by default. Every attempt to reach an unlisted host. PartialControls hostnames, not where a vendor stores data.
The NBFC stays responsible for what its LSP does.RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025 The NBFC runs a witness that verifies the LSP's ledger from signed checkpoints and hashes. Signed checkpoints, and sticky alarms on any rewrite. Evidence
Erase personal data once consent is withdrawn or the purpose is served.DPDP Act, 2023 · DPDP Rules, 2025 Nothing automatic. VICT can't reach into a vendor's systems. Every vendor that received a given borrower's data, so you know whom to ask. Not covered
Report personal data breaches to the Data Protection Board and affected people.DPDP Rules, 2025 No breach detection or reporting workflow. A tamper-evident record of which data went where, and when. Evidence

At audit time

Questions you'll be asked, and where the answer is.

Each answer is a filter over the ledger, not a document someone wrote after the fact.

Show me every vendor that received this borrower's data.
Filter the ledger for the borrower's ID and egress-allowed. Each line names the host, the purpose and the time. Blocked attempts are there too.
Prove marketing never used loan-eligibility consent.
Every attempt to call a vendor for marketing without a marketing consent is a token-denied entry. No token was issued, so no connection exists to explain.
What happened after the borrower withdrew?
The withdrawal is followed by consent-withdrawn blocks for any new call, and a tunnel-closed entry for each open connection, with the bytes sent and received before the cut.
Did any borrower data go abroad?
Allowed entries only ever name hosts on the vendor list. Attempts to reach anything else are host-not-allow-listed blocks. Whether a listed vendor stores data in India is still a contractual question.
How do we know the LSP didn't edit this log?
The NBFC's witness has held signed checkpoints of it all along. If witnessed history had changed, the witness would have raised an alarm that can't be cleared quietly. How the witness works.
